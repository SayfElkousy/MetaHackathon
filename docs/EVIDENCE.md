# Evidence table

Bibliographic data were verified on 2026-10-24 through the Europe PMC REST API, because pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov served a bot check to the audit tool. Results come from the retrieved abstracts and, where open access allowed, from the full-text XML. "Not checked" means the statement was not retrieved. It does not mean the paper lacks one.

**A publication is not a training dataset.** None of the papers below provides imaging that this project can download today.

## Not verified (open items)

- **Platelet concentration in BEAR-type repair**: searches did not return a primary study whose abstract could be verified. No platelet or biologic parameters appear anywhere in the software.
- **Repair timing**: the only verified fact is BEAR II's inclusion window (surgery ≤45 days after injury). No timing-outcome relationship is encoded.
- Titles found but not read: Murray et al. 2016 BEAR feasibility cohort (PMC5120682); Perrone et al. 2017 *Bench-to-bedside: BEAR* (PMC5729057); Kiapour et al. 2021 regional signal intensity (PMC8829819).
